City measures

2G: Tax on tobacco vaping products

tax · Passed · yes 27,159 (80%)

Sales and use tax of up to 40% of the retail price of electronic smoking devices, first-year increase $2.5 million, for licensing, health promotion, and general city services.

2H: Sales and use tax extension for open space and Long’s Gardens

tax · Passed · yes 29,450 (86%)

Extends the existing 0.15-cent transportation sales tax to Dec 31 2039 and redirects it to open-space maintenance, restoration, acquisition, and preservation, including a first-year conservation easement at Long’s…

2I: Middle-income housing program

bond · Passed · yes 23,358 (69%)

Authorizes up to $10 million of city debt (maximum repayment $15 million), without raising taxes, for permanently affordable deed restrictions and loans to middle-income households buying homes in Boulder.
