Every dollar accountable.

Downtown

Downtown has lost roughly 19% of its property value and 10% of its sales tax revenue since 2019, turning our civic and commercial heart from an economic engine to a net drag on the general fund.

Source: Better Boulder · questionnaire · 2026-09-13 · 2026 City Council Candidate Questionnaire

I think the DDA is the right tool for the job from a financial standpoint, but I also think that the city needs to overhaul much of the municipal code that makes doing business in Boulder outrageously expensive and more complex than it needs to be.

Source: Downtown Boulder Partnership · questionnaire · 2026-09-21 · 2026 Mayoral and City Council Candidate Questions — Ryan Jamieson

Governance

When you look through the handy “Budgeting for Resilience and Equity Dashboard” each department provides via PowerBI, it’s not clear that many departments have or understand their priorities.

Source: Better Boulder · questionnaire · 2026-09-13 · 2026 City Council Candidate Questionnaire

In my time in corporate spaces, I found regular meetings with key constituencies was really the simplest way to ensure that everyone was aware of what was underway and what was coming down the pipe.

Source: Downtown Boulder Partnership · questionnaire · 2026-09-21 · 2026 Mayoral and City Council Candidate Questions — Ryan Jamieson

Housing

Yes. We need the ability to build more diverse housing types by right in residential areas, plus an easier process to change zoning.

I think it’s entirely reasonable for businesses anywhere in Boulder to expect the city to enforce existing laws related to public safety.
