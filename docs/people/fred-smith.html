About this candidate

Smith has run for Boulder council four times and is now a mayoral candidate. Known as “The Grateful Fred,” he proposes a local income tax on high earners, which would require a change in state law.

  • Occupation: Laborer
  • In Boulder: moved to Boulder in the 1980s

Bio sources: boulderreportinglab.org

Boulder Reporting Lab reporter · housing
Smith proposes a 10% city income tax on household income above $184,500, which he estimates would generate $185 million annually. Because TABOR prohibits Colorado municipalities from imposing income…
Smith proposes a 10% city income tax on household income above $184,500, which he estimates would generate $185 million annually. Because TABOR prohibits Colorado municipalities from imposing income taxes, Smith proposes pursuing a statewide ballot measure to authorize such taxes. He proposes using the revenue for public facilities, childcare, affordable housing and mental health care.

Boulder Reporting Lab · 2026-08-28 · Profile source: Fred Smith
