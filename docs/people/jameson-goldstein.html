What reporters wrote

Boulder Reporting Lab reporter · housing
Goldstein is running on a platform of affordability and opposition to the city’s contract with Flock Safety, which supplies Boulder’s automatic license plate readers. He proposes an excise tax on…
Goldstein is running on a platform of affordability and opposition to the city’s contract with Flock Safety, which supplies Boulder’s automatic license plate readers. He proposes an excise tax on corporations that own more than 75 Boulder housing units and would use the revenue to subsidize rental assistance. He also supports keeping Boulder Municipal Airport open.

Boulder Reporting Lab · 2026-08-28 · Profile source: Jameson Goldstein
