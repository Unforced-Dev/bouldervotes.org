City measures

2A: City sales and use tax extension

tax · Passed · yes 24,810 (75%)

Extends the existing 0.15% city sales and use tax from Dec 31 2024 to Dec 31 2044 without raising the rate. 50% general fund (fire, public safety, homeless services, etc.), 50% arts, culture, and heritage.

2B: Elections administrative charter cleanup

charter · Passed · yes 26,137 (86%)

Amends charter sections 27, 37, 39, 46 and 57 on election administration: mayor/council petitions carried outside the municipal building, extra clerk processing time, and a clarification that state law governs charter…

302: Safe Zones 4 Kids

other · Passed · yes 20,261 (61%)

Citizen initiative. Amends city code §8-3-21 to prioritize removal of already-prohibited tents, temporary structures, and propane tanks within 500 feet of a school or 50 feet of a multi-use path or sidewalk.
