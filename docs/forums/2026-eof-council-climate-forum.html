Empower Hour: Climate and Energy Forum with Boulder City Council Candidates

October 13, 2026 · Online (Zoom webinar)

Hosted by Empower Our Future, PLAN-Boulder County.

City Council candidates only. Moderator: Chris Hoffman; host: Mary (as transcribed). Twelve of the 13 council candidates took part; the host said Benita Duran was sorry not to attend and invited questions through her website. The same three questions as the Oct. 6 mayoral forum: climate hushing, Xcel Energy, and the Valmont coal ash cleanup.
