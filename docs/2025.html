City measures

2A: Community, Culture, Resilience, and Safety (CCRS) tax extension

tax · passed

Extends the existing 0.3% CCRS sales and use tax in perpetuity (it had been set to expire Dec 31 2036) without raising the rate. Funds capital work: rec centers, fire/police stations, paths, bridges, open space…

2B: CCRS tax debt authorization

bond · passed

Authorizes up to $262 million principal ($350 million maximum repayment) payable solely from the CCRS tax extension, if 2A also passed. Companion to 2A.
