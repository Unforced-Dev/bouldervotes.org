Boulder Chamber

organization · Nonprofit business chamber; tax classification not established

https://www.boulderchamber.com/

Who they are

A nonprofit regional business chamber whose policy council and board take positions on ballot measures. Its website says it does not endorse candidates, so its 2026 graph edges are ballot positions only.

In their words: To build a strong community by supporting local for-profit and non-profit businesses, advocating for a strong business environment, and sustaining economic vitality. source

Leadership on file

  • John Tayer — President and CEO source
  • Danica Powell — Board chair source
  • Cheryl Liguori — Board vice chair source
  • Jonathan Singer — Senior director of policy programs source

Money

Offers paid business memberships, donations and sponsorship/partner tiers; member dues published from $395/year for solopreneurs on its membership page. source

How they decide

Community Affairs Council reviewed ballot measures and the Board of Directors approved positions; the Chamber explicitly states it does not endorse candidates for public office. source

What the labels on each endorsement mean
Endorser's own statement
The organization or person announced it themselves (their site, their press release, or a news story reprinting that release).
“X campaign lists Y”
The only source is the candidate's (or ballot campaign's) own website. We did not find the endorser saying it themselves. It may well be true; it is the campaign's claim.
City filing
A committee's registration with the city clerk names the candidate or measure it supports.
News listing
A news outlet lists the endorsement, but we did not find the endorser's own statement.

Titles (e.g. “U.S. Representative”) are as printed on the cited page and describe the person, not an endorsement by their office. A blank title means the source did not give one.
