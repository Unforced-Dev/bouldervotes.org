committee · City independent-expenditure-only filer (2026-IEO-025) · founded 2026

https://openboulder.org/

Who they are

City-registered independent-expenditure-only filer under the name Open Boulder. Its disclosed advertising report does not identify a candidate; no candidate edge is inferred from that spending entry.

Money

Sept. 14 report lists a $2,000 advertising expenditure to 4 Degrees Digital; it contains no contribution ledger. The city summary shows $0 contributions for this filer as of Sept. 22. source
