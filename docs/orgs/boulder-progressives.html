Boulder Progressives

organization

https://www.boulderprogressives.org/

Who they are

Boulder Progressives advocates on housing, transportation and social, racial, economic and environmental justice. It published a mayoral endorsement and six council endorsements for 2026 and registered a UCC to support them.

In their words: Boulder Progressives is committed to creating a community that is welcoming, inclusive, accessible, and safe for all. source

  • Boulder Progressives UCC is the registered candidate committee of this group. source

Money

Accepts donations; its registered 2026 UCC disclosed $2,935 in contributions from named individuals as of Sept. 24, 2026. Its separate group-wide funding was not verified. source

How they decide

Its Aug. 18 release says Richmond entered after the initial endorsement process concluded; a later release says it completed her evaluation before a full endorsement. Detailed ballot/criteria are not specified in these sources. source

2026 positions

What the labels on each endorsement mean
Endorser's own statement
The organization or person announced it themselves (their site, their press release, or a news story reprinting that release).
“X campaign lists Y”
The only source is the candidate's (or ballot campaign's) own website. We did not find the endorser saying it themselves. It may well be true; it is the campaign's claim.
City filing
A committee's registration with the city clerk names the candidate or measure it supports.
News listing
A news outlet lists the endorsement, but we did not find the endorser's own statement.

Titles (e.g. “U.S. Representative”) are as printed on the cited page and describe the person, not an endorsement by their office. A blank title means the source did not give one.
