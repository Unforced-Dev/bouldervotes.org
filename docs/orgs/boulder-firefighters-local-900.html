Boulder Association of Firefighters, IAFF Local 900

organization · IAFF local labor union · founded 1948

https://www.boulderfirefighters.org/

Who they are

IAFF Local 900 is the union for Boulder firefighters, formed in 1948. It funded the separately registered ballot committee advocating for 2L; the committee, rather than the union, has a direct ballot-position graph edge here.

In their words: The mission of the Boulder Association of Firefighters, IAFF Local 900, is grounded in our dedication to provide for and protect the men and women source

  • Boulder Firefighters for a Stronger Boulder is the ballot committee funded mainly by this group. source

Leadership on file

  • Brian Marron — President source
  • Joe Vogel — Vice President source
  • Jamie Baker — Secretary/Treasurer source

Money

Union supplied $28,669.81 cash and $684 in-kind to the 2L ballot committee in its Sept. 22 city filing. source
