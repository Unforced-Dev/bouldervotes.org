Better Boulder

organization

https://betterboulder.com/

Who they are

Better Boulder advocates on land use, housing, transportation and related local policies. Its volunteer board published a seven-person council slate and mayoral endorsement after questionnaires and a joint forum.

In their words: The mission of Better Boulder is to create vibrant, livable, and connected communities through land use planning and policy. source

Money

Receives $15 membership fees and grants from non-profit organizations supporting its community-sustainability goals; board members serve unpaid. Specific grantmakers are not named on the cited page. source

How they decide

The board reviewed 2026 mayoral and council questionnaires and participation in a collaborative candidate forum before endorsing. source

What the labels on each endorsement mean
Endorser's own statement
The organization or person announced it themselves (their site, their press release, or a news story reprinting that release).
“X campaign lists Y”
The only source is the candidate's (or ballot campaign's) own website. We did not find the endorser saying it themselves. It may well be true; it is the campaign's claim.
City filing
A committee's registration with the city clerk names the candidate or measure it supports.
News listing
A news outlet lists the endorsement, but we did not find the endorser's own statement.

Titles (e.g. “U.S. Representative”) are as printed on the cited page and describe the person, not an endorsement by their office. A blank title means the source did not give one.
