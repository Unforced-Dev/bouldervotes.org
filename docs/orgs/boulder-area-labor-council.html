Boulder Area Labor Council, AFL-CIO

organization · AFL-CIO area labor council

https://coaflcio.org/boulder-area-labor-council-afl-cio

Who they are

An AFL-CIO area labor council covering six Colorado counties, with roots it traces to the Louisville Labor Council around 1870. It issued ranked-choice mayoral recommendations, three council endorsements and positions on county ballot questions in September 2026.

In their words: The council's mission is to help working families with wage, benefit and working condition and broad working family issues. source

Leadership on file

  • Alejandra Beatty — President source
  • Dragan Mejic — Secretary-Treasurer source
  • Eric Snodgrass — Trustee source

Money

No funding information verified.

How they decide

The 2025 endorsements were announced by its Committee on Political Education (COPE); its 2026 release says it engaged and interviewed candidates. source

2026 positions

What the labels on each endorsement mean
Endorser's own statement
The organization or person announced it themselves (their site, their press release, or a news story reprinting that release).
“X campaign lists Y”
Only the candidate's (or ballot campaign's) own website says so. We didn't find the endorser saying it. It may be true, but it is the campaign's claim.
City filing
A committee's registration with the city clerk names the candidate or measure it supports.
News listing
A news outlet lists the endorsement, but we did not find the endorser's own statement.

Titles (e.g. “U.S. Representative”) are as printed on the cited page and describe the person, not an endorsement by their office. A blank title means the source did not give one.
