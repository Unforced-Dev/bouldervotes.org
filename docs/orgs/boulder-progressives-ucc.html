Boulder Progressives UCC

committee · City-registered unofficial candidate committee (2026-UCC-017) · founded 2026

https://boulderprogressives.org/

Who they are

Boulder Progressives’ separately registered committee for candidate support. Its filed purpose lists seven candidates; the city campaign-finance report discloses its individual donors and spending.

In their words: To support candidates for the offices of Mayor and City Council - Aaron Brockett, Ryan Schuchard, Jill Grano, Sam Fuqua, Tina Marquis, Rachel Isaacson, and Jamillah Richmond. source

  • This is the registered candidate committee of Boulder Progressives. source

Leadership on file

  • Eric Budd — Treasurer source
  • Kristen Eller — Co-chair source
  • Lisa Sweeney-Miran — Co-chair source

Money

City report showed $2,935 contributions and $150.21 expenditures as of filing Sept. 24, 2026; contribution detail names individual donors including candidates Aaron Brockett, Jill Grano, Sam Fuqua and Christina Marquis. source

How they decide

No published endorsement process found.

2026 positions

What the labels on each endorsement mean
Endorser's own statement
The organization or person announced it themselves (their site, their press release, or a news story reprinting that release).
“X campaign lists Y”
Only the candidate's (or ballot campaign's) own website says so. We didn't find the endorser saying it. It may be true, but it is the campaign's claim.
City filing
A committee's registration with the city clerk names the candidate or measure it supports.
News listing
A news outlet lists the endorsement, but we did not find the endorser's own statement.

Titles (e.g. “U.S. Representative”) are as printed on the cited page and describe the person, not an endorsement by their office. A blank title means the source did not give one.
