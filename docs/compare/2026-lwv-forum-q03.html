Same question, every candidate · League of Women Voters forum

Asked at the League of Women Voters of Boulder County City Council and Mayoral Candidate Forums, September 26, 2026.

How to read this. Each page puts one forum question next to every candidate we quote answering it, in the order they spoke. There are no scores and no summary of who is right.
