Same question, every candidate · League of Women Voters forum

Ballot issue 2J, the residential vacancy excise tax, would be a tax of approximately $4,000 on residential property vacant for more than half the year. Do you support this tax and will it generate enough income to pay for administering this complex program? (30 seconds)

Asked at the League of Women Voters of Boulder County City Council and Mayoral Candidate Forums, September 26, 2026.
