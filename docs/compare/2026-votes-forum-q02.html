Title 9 of our city code is our land use code. Some council and community members are advocating for a complete rewrite of this title. Do you support or oppose this, and do you think now is the right time to undertake this effort?

Asked at the Collaborative City Council and Mayoral Candidate Forum (PLAN-Boulder County / Open Boulder / Better Boulder), September 2, 2026.

How to read this. Each page puts one forum question next to every candidate we quote answering it, in the order their names appear on the ballot. There are no scores and no summary of who is right.
