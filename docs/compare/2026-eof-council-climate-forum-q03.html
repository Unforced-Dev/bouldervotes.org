Same question, every candidate · Empower Our Future council climate forum

Xcel says it will deliver 100% clean electricity by 2050, not Boulder's 2030 goal, and has presented no concrete plan under the 2020 franchise agreement. What remedies would you propose for Xcel's failures to deliver, at the city level, at the PUC, or at the state level? And would you be open to inviting cities that have gone to market for their electricity to come to Boulder to educate us about life after Xcel?

Asked at the Empower Hour: Climate and Energy Forum with Boulder City Council Candidates, October 13, 2026.
