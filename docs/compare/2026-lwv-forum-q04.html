Same question, every candidate · League of Women Voters forum

Boulder voters are being asked to authorize $400 million in new debt through additional property taxes for recreation, public safety and other city facilities as part of ballot issue 2K. Do you support this measure? Why or why not?

Asked at the League of Women Voters of Boulder County City Council and Mayoral Candidate Forums, September 26, 2026.
